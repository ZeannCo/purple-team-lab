Password reset: how employees reset a forgotten Contoso password.

1. Go to https://passwordreset.contoso.local from any company device.
2. Verify with the authenticator app registered during onboarding.
3. Choose a new password of at least 14 characters. Reusing the last 10 passwords is blocked.
If the authenticator app is lost, the helpdesk can issue a temporary access pass after an ID check.
